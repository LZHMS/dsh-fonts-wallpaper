This plugin bundles a subset of the following open source font:

  Font:     LXGW WenKai GB Screen (霞鹜文楷 GB 屏幕阅读版)
  Project:  https://github.com/lxgw/LxgwWenKai-CJKVersion
  License:  SIL Open Font License 1.1 (full text below)
  Subset:   GB2312 (6,768 common Han characters) + Latin and punctuation,
            converted to WOFF2 with fontTools. Glyph outlines are unchanged.

On machines where the full font is installed, the plugin uses the installed
copy (src: local(...) is tried before the bundled subset).
